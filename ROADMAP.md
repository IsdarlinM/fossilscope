# Roadmap

## Current — 0.2.x hardening
Temporal graph, time snapshots, lifecycle states, historical API diff, confidence decay, clustering/acquisition lineage and passive local-source adapters are implemented.

Next:
- Pluggable passive collectors for explicitly configured CT/DNS/archive/repository/package providers with caching, quotas and source terms documented.
- Temporal graph virtualization for large histories and better source-independence scoring.
- Historical identity/OAuth/SDK evolution diffing and stale-reference re-observation workflows.
- Cross-project correlation rules with Exposure DNA and TrustBoundary through versioned SRIC exports.

## 1.0
Stable temporal schemas, source adapter SDK, reproducible archaeology datasets and independently validated fossil scoring.
